Enrollment

A warm welcome to Sunflower Garden Nursery School.

Our program is designed for families seeking a peaceful, relationship-based setting where children can grow within familiar caregiving relationships, consistent daily rhythms, and a beautiful home environment.

Children & Continuity

For children from 3 months through kindergarten age.

Sunflower Garden Nursery School welcomes children from 3 months through kindergarten age into a nurturing, home-based environment inspired by the principles of Waldorf Early Childhood Education and RIE®.

Through continuity of care, children develop confidence, independence, emotional security, and a genuine love of learning, creating a strong foundation for their transition to preschool or kindergarten when they are developmentally ready.

What Our Program Includes

  • Homemade, nutritious breakfast, lunch, and afternoon snacks prepared with fresh, wholesome ingredients
  • A warm, nurturing home environment with a low teacher-to-child ratio
  • Daily outdoor exploration in all seasons
  • Waldorf-inspired activities including watercolor painting, storytelling, music, gardening, baking, practical life, handcrafts, and seasonal celebrations
  • Respectful, relationship-based care inspired by RIE® principles
  • Exposure to English, Spanish, and Portuguese through songs, stories, conversations, and everyday interactions
  • Ongoing communication and partnership with families to support each child's development

Enrollment Agreement

Consistency helps hold the small community.

To provide consistency for every child and maintain the stability of our small community, enrollment is offered on a month-to-month basis.

We kindly ask families to provide 60 days' written notice if they plan to withdraw their child from the program. This notice allows us to thoughtfully support each child's transition while maintaining continuity for the children, families, and educators within our community.
