Daily Life

A Typical Day

A familiar day, with room for each child.

Time to Play & Create

Art, gardening, baking, yoga, and uninterrupted free play give children many ways to explore, imagine, and create.

Songs, Stories & Language

Circle time brings songs, movement, finger plays, stories, and seasonal verses. English, Spanish, and Portuguese are woven naturally into the day.

Food, Rest & Connection

Children share homemade meals around the community table, then settle into a peaceful rest period adapted to individual needs.

Activities

Work, play, and wonder woven through the week.

Watercolor painting Gardening Baking Food preparation Nature crafts Yoga Music Sensory play Seasonal projects Free play

Food & Community

Made with care, shared together.

Meals are prepared with fresh, wholesome ingredients and the warmth of traditional Brazilian home cooking. Children and teachers eat together, practice conversation and table manners, and enjoy food without pressure.

Children also help wash produce, mix ingredients, bake, and set the table when possible. These simple tasks build confidence and practical life skills.

Outdoor Life

Outside in every season.

Outdoor play is an essential part of every day. Whenever possible, children spend time exploring nature, climbing, digging, gardening, and discovering the world around them.

On rainy days, children put on their rain gear and delight in splashing through puddles.
