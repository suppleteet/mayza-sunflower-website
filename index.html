Sunflower Garden Nursery School

Our Mission & Philosophy

A gentle, unhurried childhood rooted in relationship, respect, and wonder.

Sunflower Garden Nursery School provides loving, individualized care in a peaceful, nurturing environment inspired by the principles of Waldorf Early Childhood Education and RIE® (Resources for Infant Educarers).

We believe that the earliest years of life deserve meaningful relationships, consistent daily rhythms, and a warm home environment where children develop security, confidence, and belonging.

Our mission is to protect the magic of childhood while laying a strong foundation for a lifetime of learning, compassion, creativity, and well-being.

At a Glance

  • Children from 3 months through kindergarten age
  • Waldorf-inspired beauty, simplicity, rhythm, and nature
  • Respectful care guided by RIE principles
  • English, Spanish, and Portuguese woven into daily life
  • Homemade meals and abundant outdoor time

Whole-Child Care

Body, mind, spirit, and the quiet confidence of belonging.

Waldorf-Inspired Care

Beauty, simplicity, seasonal celebrations, artistic expression, and time in nature help imagination and joyful learning flourish.

Respectful RIE Care

Attentive caregiving, authentic communication, and freedom of movement support each child as capable and competent.

Wholesome Nutrition

Homemade meals are prepared with fresh ingredients and shared around a peaceful community table.

Outdoor Life & Nature

Children climb, dig, garden, explore the seasons, and spend generous unhurried time outdoors.

Living Community

Continuity of care helps children build resilience, self-confidence, strong relationships, and a love of learning.

Our Home

A warm, home-based space made for wonder, play, and rest.
